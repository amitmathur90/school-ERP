Part One

Start Here

Logging in

The sign-in screen has four tabs. Pick the one that matches who you are — the app then reads your actual account role from the server and sends you to the right dashboard automatically.

TabWho it's forYou sign in with
StudentApplicants and enrolled studentsEmail address + password
ParentA parent or guardian linked to one or more studentsEmail address + password
StaffFaculty, Accounts, LibrarianEmail address + password
AdministratorAdmin and Super AdminUsername + password

Under the Staff tab, every non-teaching role — Accounts, Librarian — signs in the same way Faculty does. The server looks up your actual role and opens the matching dashboard; the tab is just where you start, not what decides your permissions.

Staff accounts aren't self-service — there's no sign-up form for the Staff or Administrator tabs; an Admin or Super Admin has to create your account first (Faculty & Staff → Add). Parent accounts are usually created automatically the moment a student submits their admission form (using the father's email and phone from Family Details) — see Parent — but Admin can also add one by hand from the Parents screen if needed. If you don't have credentials yet, ask your Admin or the school office.

A few things happen automatically based on your account's status:

  • A deactivated staff or admin account is blocked at login with a clear message — contact your Admin to reactivate it.
  • If you're a student whose application is still a draft, logging in drops you straight back into the admission wizard where you left off, instead of a dashboard.
  • If your application is pending review or was rejected, you'll see a read-only status screen instead of the full student dashboard — the dashboard only unlocks once an Admin approves you.

Applying for admission

Prospective students apply through a five-step form — no account needed until the very end. Click Apply for Admission under the Student tab on the sign-in screen to start.

  1. Basic & Personal Details

    Name, date of birth, gender, email, phone, emergency and WhatsApp numbers, Aadhar, a photo and signature upload, and the password you'll use to sign in later. Email and phone are checked for uniqueness as you type.

  2. Address

    Permanent address, contact number, state/city/PIN, domicile state, and an optional separate current address.

  3. Family Details

    Father's and mother's name, phone, email, and occupation; guardian details if applicable. If you fill in the father's email and phone, a parent portal login is created automatically when you submit — see Parent.

  4. Education & Class

    The course/class you're applying to, medium of instruction, and — unless you're applying to Nursery or Play Group — your last school, exam year, and result.

  5. Academic Details & Documents

    An editable table of every qualifying exam you've passed, plus scanned copies of your documents (JPG, PNG, or GIF, under 5 MB each). Each academic record needs a matching uploaded document. You'll also tick a Terms & Conditions box and, if online payment is enabled, pay the admission fee here before you can submit.

Save each step before moving on. "Save Step" writes your progress to the server as a draft — you can log out and come back later, and logging back in will drop you right back into the form where you left off. "Next" is blocked until the current step is saved.

After the final submit you'll see a confirmation screen with a preview of the registration email, and a button to go sign in. From there you can track your application's status — see Logging in for what a pending or rejected application looks like.

Reviewing applications Admin

Every submitted application lands in Admissions Registry, sorted into Pending, Approved, and Rejected tabs — filterable by class and searchable by name, email, or phone.

  • Open View on any row for the full application, including the Academic Details table and every uploaded document.
  • Approve assigns a roll number automatically and moves the student to the Students directory.
  • Reject requires a short reason — the applicant sees this reason on their status screen.
  • Select multiple rows to bulk-delete applications, or bring in a batch of existing applicants with Import CSV.

Parent

Parent

Read-only, by design — a parent can see everything about their own linked child, but can't issue a book, mark attendance, record a payment, or edit anything. If you have more than one child at the school, a switcher at the top of every page lets you pick which one you're viewing.

How the login gets created

Whenever an applicant fills in the father's email and phone in the admission form's Family Details step and submits, a parent login is created automatically the moment they hit Final Submit:

  • Username — the father's email address.
  • Password — the father's phone number, exactly as entered.
  • A welcome email is sent (in-app log + real email if SMTP is configured) with these credentials.

If a second child is admitted later using the same father's email, they're automatically added to the existing login instead of creating a duplicate — one parent account, both children. If that email is already in use by a teacher or student account, auto-creation is skipped and Admin needs to sort it out by hand from the Parents screen. Either way, Admin can also create or edit a parent login manually at any time — see Super Admin & Admin.

Every child's data is checked server-side, not just hidden in the menu. A parent account can only ever pull records for the specific student(s) it's linked to — asking for another student's data by ID is rejected outright, the same principle applied everywhere else access control matters in this app.

There's no in-app "change password" screen for parents yet — if you want to change the auto-assigned password (your phone number), ask the school office to reset it from the Parents screen.

Part Three

Core Workflows

Fees & payments

Available to Admin, Accounts, and (view level) Faculty for staff-side entry; students pay their own balance online.

Recording a payment (staff)

Cash or offline payments are entered directly against a student's ledger from Fees & Receipts, including EMI plans (installment amount, tenure, how many installments are paid vs. remaining). Every payment generates a unique transaction ID, a printable receipt, and a simulated email receipt.

Paying online (student)

From Fees & Payments, a student can choose Pay Online or Pay EMI Now. Two gateways are supported, but only one is active school-wide at a time — Admin switches between them from Fees:

GatewayHow it worksBest for
RazorpayOpens a popup for cards, UPI, or netbanking — the browser never leaves the app.Works out of the box, including on a local/testing setup.
PayU MoneyRedirects the browser to PayU's own payment page, then back to the school's server.Needs the server reachable from the public internet to receive the result.

A student can pay their admission fee even before their application is approved — it's recorded against their record and reconciled automatically once Admin approves them.

Due-date reminders

Anyone with an outstanding balance due within 30 days gets an automatic dashboard notification and email once a day. Admin can also trigger this on demand from Fees → Send Reminders Now instead of waiting for the daily check. A student won't be reminded again for 7 days after their last reminder, so it doesn't nag.

Attendance Faculty

Pick a class and a date — every student defaults to Present, and you tap to flip anyone to Absent before saving. Students see their own record as a running percentage, flagged if it falls below 75%.

Grades & results Faculty

Create a result set per student by semester and exam type, entering subject-by-subject marks against each subject's maximum. Students see their results grouped by semester and exam type, with a detail view showing subject-wise marks, totals, and overall percentage.

Notices

Admin and Faculty can both post to the school-wide Notice Board — Admin can delete any notice; Faculty can post but not delete. Every notice shows who posted it and their role. Everyone, including students, sees the board in chronological order.

Messaging a student directly AdminHODFaculty

For a one-off note to a specific student — a reminder, an instruction, anything that doesn't belong on the public Notice Board.

From Students (Admin/HOD) or My Students (Faculty), click Message next to any student's row, write a note, and send. It lands directly in that student's Notifications inbox, signed with your name — there's no reply-back thread; a student responds the normal way (email, in person) rather than through the app.

Who a message says it's from is always the signed-in staff member sending it — the server derives your name and role itself rather than trusting anything the browser sends, so there's no way to send a note that looks like it's from someone else.

Leave requests

Accounts and Librarian can each apply for their own leave from My Leave — pick a type, dates, and a reason. Admin/Super Admin sees every request and approves or rejects it with an optional note.

Faculty accounts don't have a My Leave tab yet — see the note under Faculty.

Library

A real catalog and circulation system, not just a spec — scoped to what a school library actually needs day-to-day.

At the desk LibrarianAdmin

Catalog
Add, edit, or remove titles and copies; bulk-load a collection with Import CSV, matched by accession number.
Issue & Return
Issue a copy to a student or teacher by accession number, process returns, and mark a fine paid or waived.
Reports
Overdue list, currently-issued list, most-borrowed titles, reading-program participation, and a full catalog export.
Settings
Per grade-band policy — loan period, max simultaneous loans, whether overdue books trigger a borrowing hold or a fine (with rate and cap), and the renewal limit. Editable by Admin/Super Admin; Librarian sees it read-only.

Browsing & borrowing StudentFaculty

Search the catalog by title, author, or reading level, and see how many copies are available. There's no self-checkout — bring the book, or its accession number, to the librarian's desk to have it issued. Once issued, it shows up under My Loans with its due date; you can renew it yourself as long as it isn't overdue and you're under the renewal limit.

Students additionally get a Reading Log — a running count of completed books with milestone badges at 5, 10, 20, 50, and 100 books read.

Borrowing rules follow your grade band automatically (younger grades typically get shorter, simpler terms); teachers borrow under one fixed staff policy — 30-day loans, 5 books at a time, no fines.

Support / helpdesk

Any student can open a ticket — a subject, a message, and an optional attachment (JPG, PNG, GIF, or PDF, under 5 MB) — and reply in the thread afterward. Staff with helpdesk access see every ticket, can reply (which reopens a resolved or closed one), and set its status. A staff reply automatically shows up in the student's Notifications.

Reports center AdminAccounts

Seven report types, each filterable and exportable to CSV, and formatted to print cleanly:

  • Admission Report
  • Student Report
  • Fee Report
  • EMI Report
  • Pending Fee
  • Class-Wise Report
  • Daily Collection

Importing data by CSV Admin

Every "Import CSV" button uses the same forgiving importer — column headers are matched flexibly, nothing is silently dropped, and any column that doesn't match a known field is kept as "Additional Information" instead of being discarded.

  • Admissions Registry — bulk-load applications/students.
  • Students directory — student records.
  • Fee Ledger — fee and transaction rows.
  • Library Catalog — book titles and copies, matched by accession number so re-importing an updated list updates existing entries instead of duplicating them.

Good to Know

What isn't built yet

So you don't go looking for it. None of these are bugs — they're simply out of scope for this version.

  • ✕
    No student ID card feature.
  • ✕
    Faculty can't apply for their own leave through the app yet, unlike Accounts and Librarian.
  • ✕
    Library reservations and barcode/RFID scanning aren't built — issuing is by accession number, and there's no hold queue for a book that's currently out.
